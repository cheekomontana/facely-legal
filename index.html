Worth knowing up front

Facely analyses photos of your face using Google's Gemini AI to produce a score and a daily routine. Your photos are never stored on our servers — they are sent, analysed and discarded, and the only lasting copy stays on your device. Accounts are anonymous: we never ask for your name, email or password.

A Facely score is an AI-generated opinion, not a medical assessment and not a fact about your worth. See the Terms for what that means in practice.
